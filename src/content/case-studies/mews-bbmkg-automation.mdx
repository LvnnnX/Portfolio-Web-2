## Problem

BBMKG Wilayah III Denpasar produces nine daily administrative documents — each manually filled from data scattered across observation logs, internal spreadsheets, and email reports. The result: hours of duplicated typing per day, with predictable transcription errors. As a six-week intern, I owned end-to-end delivery: requirement analysis, design, build, deploy, train.

## Approach

1. **User research** — sat with operational staff for the first week to map the actual document workflow vs. the documented one. Found 3 "shadow" steps that needed to be folded in.
2. **Single source of truth** — Flask backend that pulls from the existing data sources and merges into a unified daily payload.
3. **One-click generation** — the staff selects a date, picks a document type, fills any fields the system can't derive, and exports the rendered output.
4. **Training & handoff** — two on-site training sessions, plus a written runbook so the platform survives my exit.

## Results

- **9 documents automated** end-to-end.
- **Six-week build** from kickoff to handoff, including training.
- Platform **still in active daily use** at the institution 2+ years later — the most durable artifact of any internship I've shipped.
