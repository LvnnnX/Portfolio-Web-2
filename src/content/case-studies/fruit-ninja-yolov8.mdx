## Problem

Existing Fruit Ninja bots rely on color thresholding or template matching — brittle against lighting, occlusion, and the bomb-vs-fruit confusion that ends a run instantly. The challenge: prove that a modern object detector can deliver **frame-rate-aware** inference on consumer hardware while staying robust enough to survive Survival Mode.

## Approach

1. **Frame capture** — `bettercam` for sub-millisecond DXGI capture, bypassing the GDI bottleneck that caps PrintScreen-style approaches at ~30 FPS.
2. **Detector** — YOLOv8 family. Benchmarked **13 variants** across the n/s/m/l/x scales and image sizes (320, 416, 640) to find the FPS-vs-mAP frontier.
3. **Post-processing** — class-aware NMS plus a velocity tracker so the bot swipes ahead of the fruit, not at it.
4. **Action layer** — `pydirectinput` for mouse path interpolation; bezier curves to mimic human swipes.
