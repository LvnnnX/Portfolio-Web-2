## Problem

School-based online exams routinely fail under three pressures: students freezing the client clock with devtools, copying questions to other tabs, and crashing under exam-day load. SMA Negeri 2 Amlapura needed something that survived all three without paying for an enterprise LMS.

## Approach

1. **Zero-trust client** — every authoritative state lives in Supabase RPC. Client-side timer is purely UI; the actual exam clock ticks server-side.
2. **JIT question fetch** — questions are requested one-at-a-time, never bulk-loaded, so a leaked LocalStorage dump is incomplete by design.
3. **AES-256 LocalStorage** — what little is cached client-side (current question, partial answers) is encrypted with a per-session key derived from the auth token.
4. **Survival Mode** — 3-lives variant for low-stakes practice; built on the same RPC primitives.
5. **Admin dashboard** — Rich-text + LaTeX question authoring, real-time progress monitoring, automated result analytics.
